Accessibility and classroom rules
• Text targets at least 4.5:1 contrast on its panel; large type at least 3:1.
• Real buttons, labels and inputs; every control at least 44 px tall.
• Status uses words as well as color (Available, Restricted, Needs auth, Unavailable).
• Classroom view scales the interface by about 12% and can request full screen.
• Sample results are always labeled illustrative; no classroom backend is simulated.
